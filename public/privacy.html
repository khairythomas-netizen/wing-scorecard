WingZ

Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

WingZ is an app for rating chicken wings. It is run by an individual, not a company. This page explains exactly what the app collects, where it goes and what it is used for. If anything here is unclear, email wingz_app@outlook.com.

What WingZ collects

  • Your account. Your email address, and a password which is stored only as a hash. If you sign in with Google instead, WingZ receives your email address, your name and your profile picture from Google. Nothing else, and never your Google password.
  • Your profile. The username you choose, plus any display name, bio and avatar you add.
  • What you post. Your reviews and their scores, the restaurant and flavour, what you ordered, the price if you enter one, the spice level, your notes and your photos.
  • What you do in the app. Who you follow, and the posts you like, comment on, save or mark as want-to-try.

Location

If you allow it, WingZ asks your browser for your position so it can show restaurants near you and rank search results by distance. Your position is kept on your own device and is sent to the map and search services below as a hint about where to look. WingZ does not store your position on its servers, and does not keep a history of where you have been. Refusing the prompt does not break the app: searches simply stop being sorted by distance.

Who else can see your data

Reviews you publish publicly are visible to anyone using WingZ, along with your username, avatar and the photos on those reviews. Setting your account to private limits your posts to people you approve.

Beyond that, WingZ uses a small number of outside services to work at all:

  • Supabase stores the accounts, posts and photos, and handles signing in.
  • GitHub Pages serves the app itself.
  • Photon, run by Komoot, and Nominatim, run by OpenStreetMap, answer restaurant and address searches.
  • Esri serves the map images.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google.

Each of these receives only what it needs to answer the request in front of it. Your search text and an approximate position go to the search services; nothing about your account or your reviews does.

What WingZ does not do

There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no third-party trackers of any kind in this app. Your data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone for marketing.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete any post you have made, at any time.
  • You can make your account private from your profile.
  • You can ask for your account and everything in it to be deleted by emailing wingz_app@outlook.com.

How long things are kept

Your posts stay until you delete them or ask for your account to be removed. Deleting a post removes the post and its photos.

Children

WingZ is not intended for anyone under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
